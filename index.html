The one rule

Nothing goes in that you didn't say. No stock lines, no sentiment you don't feel, no tidy ending you didn't mean. The whole point is that it's them, and that it's you saying it.

Afterward, if there's something else you want to put into words, for them or for someone still here, that can be its own conversation. No pressure either way.
